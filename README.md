### What I'm building

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[SuVerse](https://www.suverse.io)** | When customers ask ChatGPT or Claude who to call, SuVerse shows a local business who gets named — and gives it an AI-readable card (prices, hours, area, contacts) so it has a chance to be among them. Website, iOS app and Telegram bot. |
| **[PingPoint](https://pingpoint.suverse.io/docs)** | Real-time freight tracking for US truckload shipments — live driver GPS and ETA for brokers, carriers and AI agents. |
| **[SuVerse Pay](https://github.com/sudzikcoin/suverse-pay)** | Self-serve x402 payment infrastructure: pay-per-call APIs for AI agents, USDC on Base, Solana and Cosmos, non-custodial. |
